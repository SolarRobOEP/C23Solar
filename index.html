Why solar?

A simple way to cut your energy bills.

Panels make the power, a battery keeps it for later, and you decide how much you still need from the grid.

Take back control

Every unit you generate is one you don’t have to buy. The more of your own power you use, the less exposed you are when energy prices climb — and the more predictable your bills become.

Lower bills, year after year

Panels have no moving parts and keep generating for decades. Add a battery and the daytime surplus runs your evenings too, when most homes use the most.

Get paid for your surplus

Under the Smart Export Guarantee, licensed energy suppliers pay you for electricity you send back to the grid. We’ll explain how to compare export tariffs once you’re switched on.

Quick, tidy installation

Once your design is agreed, we book the work on fixed dates, handle the paperwork and grid notification, and leave the site as we found it.

FAQs

Questions people ask us first.

How do I get started?

Fill in the free quote form — it takes about a minute. We’ll get in touch to talk through your options, then arrange a survey so your quote is based on your actual roof and usage.

Who can get solar from C23 Solar?

Homeowners and businesses who own their property. If you rent or lease, you’ll need your landlord’s written permission before anything can be installed. Flats are often unsuitable because of shared roofs, but tell us your situation and we’ll let you know.

Do solar panels work on cloudy days?

Yes. Panels generate from daylight, not just direct sunshine. Output is lower when it’s overcast and in winter, and higher on long, bright days — which is why estimates are worked out across a whole year.

Do I need a battery?

No, panels work without one. A battery lets you store surplus daytime generation and use it in the evening, so you buy less from the grid. Whether it’s worthwhile depends on when you use electricity; we’ll show you figures both ways.

Do I need planning permission?

Most home installations in the UK fall under permitted development, so they don’t. Exceptions can include listed buildings, conservation areas and some flat roofs. We check this as part of your survey.

What is the Smart Export Guarantee?

It’s a UK scheme under which licensed energy suppliers pay you for electricity you export to the grid. Rates vary between suppliers and tariffs, so it’s worth comparing them once your system is installed.
