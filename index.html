04 — After dark

Store the day. Spend it at night.

Most homes use the most electricity just as their panels stop producing. A battery closes that gap — charging from the midday surplus, then running the evening on sunshine you already caught.

  • Use more of what you generate
  • Lean on the grid less at peak times
  • Keep essentials running through an outage, with backup-ready systems
