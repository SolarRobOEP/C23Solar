FAQs

Do you install solar panels across Warrington?

Yes. We survey and install across Warrington, including Great Sankey, Penketh, Lymm, Stockton Heath, Birchwood, Culcheth, Appleton and Padgate. Send a free quote request with your postcode and we’ll confirm the details.

Do I need planning permission for solar panels in Warrington?

Most homes don’t, because roof-mounted panels usually count as permitted development. Listed buildings, some conservation areas and some flat roofs are exceptions, and those are decided by Warrington Borough Council. We check this for you as part of the survey.

How long does installation take?

Most home installations are completed in one or two days once scaffolding is up. We agree the dates with you in advance and handle the grid notification.

Can I add a battery to panels I already have?

In most cases, yes. We look at your existing inverter and system during the survey and recommend a battery that works with it.
